# Weekly diary: how to use it

The weekly diary builds the "diary" part of your members' email from the church calendar, so
you do not have to type it out each week. Only people you give access to can see it. The
notes you add are never shown on the public website and are never sent to Google.

## Logging in

1. Go to `https://new.moortownbaptistchurch.org.uk/wp-login.php` (the address will change when the new
   website goes live; John will tell you).
2. Type your username and password and press **Log In**. You go straight to the Weekly diary.
3. If you forget your password, press **Lost your password?** on the login page and follow the email.
4. The left-hand menu shows only **Weekly diary** and **Profile**. The bar along the top has your name:
   use it to open **Profile** (change your password there) or to **Log out**.
5. When you have finished, log out from the top bar, especially on a shared computer.

You cannot change the website's pages, posts or settings with this login; that is deliberate, so
nothing can be broken by accident. Ask John if you need something else.

## Each week

1. Log in (see "Logging in" below). You arrive at **Weekly diary**; if you are ever elsewhere, choose **Weekly diary** in the menu on the left.
2. The dates are already set to the coming Sunday and the Sunday after. To use other dates,
   change **From** and **To (included)** and press **Show**. (A range can be up to 31 days.)
3. Press **Refresh from the calendar** if someone has just changed the calendar and you want
   the very latest. Otherwise the page may be up to 15 minutes behind.
4. Look through **This week** (everything in those dates) and **Coming up** (one-off events in
   the six weeks after, repeating events left out).
5. Add or change a note under any line if you want one. A note is saved as soon as you click
   out of the box ("Saved." appears).
6. Press **Copy for email**, then paste into your email (Gmail or Outlook). It pastes as ordinary
   paragraphs. If the button cannot copy, use the plain text box underneath and copy from there.

## Notes

- A note on a **repeating** event (Lunch Club, say) is kept and shows again every week until you
  change or clear it. You only have to write it once.
- A note on a **one-off** event belongs to that event only.
- To remove a note, clear the box.
- **Notes no longer used** at the bottom lists notes for events that are no longer in the
  calendar (for example a group that has stopped). Press **Delete note** to tidy them away.

## What a line looks like

`Wednesday 21st October Lunch Club 10.00 - 1.30pm. Soup and a roll. Your note here.`

- The date, the event name and the time come from the calendar.
- A place is added ("at Roundhay Park Cafe") only when the event is somewhere other than the church.
- Then the first sentence of the calendar description, if there is one, then your note.
- Events cancelled in the calendar do not appear.
- If you want to change the wording of a line, change the event in the calendar (or put your
  wording in the note).

## How times are written

| Calendar | Diary |
|---|---|
| 7pm to 8pm | `7.00 - 8.00pm` |
| 10am to 11.30am | `10.00 - 11.30am` |
| 10am to 1.30pm | `10.00 - 1.30pm` |
| 10am to noon | `10.00 - 12.00 midday` |
| 10pm to midnight | `10.00 - 12.00 midnight` |
| Starts at 7.30pm, no end | `7.30pm` |
| Starts at 10am, no end | `10.00am` |
| Starts at noon, no end | `midday` |
| All day | no time |

Minutes are always shown, and am or pm is written once, after the end time. An event over
several days shows its start time and "until Sunday 1st November".

## If something looks wrong

- A yellow notice says the calendar could not be reached: the page is showing the last copy it
  saved, which may be out of date. Try **Refresh** later.
- An event is missing: check that it is in the "MBC Events" Google calendar and not cancelled.
  An event that started before your first date is not listed, even if it is still going on.
